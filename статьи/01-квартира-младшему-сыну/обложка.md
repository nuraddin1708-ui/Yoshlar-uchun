# Обложка: 5 промптов (задание 4)

Статья: «Свекровь подарила квартиру младшему сыну. А к врачам возить — невестке»
Раздел: Рассказы и истории / Семейные драмы · формат Дзена 16:9

Общий хвост для всех промптов (Stable Diffusion / SDXL / Midjourney):
`photorealistic, 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, warm cinematic light, faces large and close to camera, no text, no watermark`

Negative prompt (SD):
`cartoon, anime, 3d render, deformed hands, extra fingers, blurry face, text, logo, watermark, oversaturated`

---

## Вариант А — «Тост» (рекомендую)
Russian woman, 44, light brown hair in a simple bun, beige cardigan, at a festive home dinner table, looking sideways with quiet hurt; in soft focus behind her an elderly Russian woman, 72, in a festive blouse looks away guiltily, a smiling man, 39, raises a glass of wine; salads, layered Napoleon cake, chrysanthemums, evening lamplight in a Soviet-era apartment.

## Вариант Б — «Ты знал?»
Close-up of a Russian married couple in their 40s in a car at night, woman in the passenger seat turns to her husband with a steady questioning look, man grips the steering wheel and stares straight ahead avoiding her eyes, streetlight reflections on the windshield, tense silence.

## Вариант В — «Календарь»
Close-up of a Russian woman's hand in an apron crossing out a red handwritten note "вторник 11:00" on a paper calendar on a fridge door, her face in the background slightly blurred, determined and calm, morning kitchen light.

## Вариант Г — «Свекровь и невестка»
Two Russian women at a small kitchen table, elderly woman, 72, neatly combed grey hair, festive cardigan, holding a teacup and looking down with remorse; younger woman, 44, looking at her with reserved warmth; cabbage pie on the table, old cuckoo clock on the wall, soft window light.

## Вариант Д — «Ключи»
Extreme close-up of a woman's palm holding a ring of old apartment keys with a small hand-painted wooden cuckoo keychain, blurred Russian apartment hallway behind, warm winter evening light, emotional, nostalgic.
